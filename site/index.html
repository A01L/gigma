A design editor you already know how to use

The same panels, tools and shortcuts as Figma, running on your own machine. Plus a few things an online editor cannot do.

Spec nodes. A task card with ports: materials come in on the left, the frames to work in go out on the right. The AI knows what to build and where.

Works with your AI.
On your canvas.

A built-in MCP server with 60+ tools. Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code and LM Studio connect to the running app and draw, read and fix the design. Every step is an ordinary, undoable edit.
